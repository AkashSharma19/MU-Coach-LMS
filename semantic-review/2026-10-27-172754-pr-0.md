# Unified communications feed remains behaviorally incomplete

The change integrates Communication > Announcements with post management, a two-step composer, delivery controls, analytics, and student preview. Earlier focus, acknowledgement-idempotency, attachment-validation, and clipboard-reporting defects are addressed, but core publication and preview rules remain broken. Watch for: **confirmed** publication validation bypass; **confirmed** scheduling and expiry inconsistencies; **confirmed** audience corruption; **confirmed** pinned-dismissal and share-analytics defects.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

Drafts can be saved without an audience, then published directly from the table without composer validation (**confirmed**). Scheduling never delivers at the configured time, and pinned expiry can precede scheduled publication (**confirmed**).

New posts retain structured targeting, but seeded multi-cohort and club-only audiences are reconstructed lossily during edit (**confirmed**). Student preview moves dismissed and overflow pinned notices into activity without acknowledgement controls, while successful shares do not update analytics (**confirmed**).

<details><summary>Details</summary>

## Publication paths bypass delivery constraints

`savePost(true)` skips step-two validation, while the table offers Publish now for every Draft and Scheduled row and `setPostStatus` performs no validation (**confirmed**, `src/AnnouncementsPage.tsx:142-158,183`). A draft saved on step one can become Published with `No audience selected`. Route Publish now through full publication validation.

`computedStatus` never promotes a Scheduled post when `publishDate` arrives, and the UI explicitly keeps it scheduled until manual publication (**confirmed**, `src/AnnouncementsPage.tsx:107,186`). Add due-time delivery/status processing.

Pinned expiry is checked against now independently of scheduled publication (**confirmed**, `src/AnnouncementsPage.tsx:135-145`). A notice scheduled for tomorrow may expire today; validate expiry against the effective publication time.

## Existing audience hierarchy is not round-trippable

Seeded posts omit `targeting`, so `openEdit` reconstructs hierarchy from display text with single-value ternaries (**confirmed**, `src/AnnouncementsPage.tsx:129-134`). The Cohorts 7–8 post restores only Cohort 7, while a Consulting Club-only post gains the first program. Seed or migrate structured targeting and remove display-string inference.

## Pinned preview state breaks acknowledgement and analytics

The preview takes at most two pinned posts, then treats every post outside that resulting list as activity (**confirmed**, `src/AnnouncementsPage.tsx:203-205`). Dismissed and overflow pinned notices therefore reappear as ordinary cards, where required acknowledgement is unavailable. Exclude all pinned posts from activity and model dismissal independently.

The share action copies successfully but never increments `post.shares` (**confirmed**, `src/AnnouncementsPage.tsx:173-174,205`). Increment the metric only after successful sharing so the table and analytics drawer reflect the interaction.
