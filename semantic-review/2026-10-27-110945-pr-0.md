# Competition management workspace with group awards

The change makes Student Experience → Competitions the default landing view when no academic deep link is restored, and adds searchable card/table competition browsing, competition and group CRUD, award workflows, reward configuration, group award history, and an award-log drawer. The implementation keeps all groups, awards, totals, and reward policy in one page-level store rather than attaching them to a competition, while some advertised reward controls do not govern award submission. **Watch for:** **confirmed** cross-competition data leakage and incorrect counts; **confirmed** reward limits and approval being cosmetic; **confirmed** keyboard users being unable to open a competition detail or remain contained in its dialogs; **confirmed** group deletion leaving orphaned award records.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

Competition selection changes only `selectedCompetitionId`; groups, awards, and reward settings remain global. Consequently every competition—including a newly created competition whose list card says zero groups—opens onto the same four groups, points, award history, and policy, and group CRUD never updates the list-level counts (**confirmed**).

The award form validates only that the unsigned point value is positive. Configured minimum/maximum awards and the “Require approval” workflow do not participate in submission, so the Rewards tab promises controls the award path bypasses (**confirmed**).

Both competition view modes use pointer-only container click handlers to enter detail: an `<article>` in card mode and a `<tr>` in compact mode. Neither is focusable or keyboard-activated, leaving keyboard-only users without a route to the groups and rewards tabs (**confirmed**).

Deleting a group removes only the group object, not its award entries. Those records continue contributing to global award counts and logs while the deleted group disappears from the log filter options, creating an inconsistent audit surface (**confirmed**).

<details>
<summary>Issues (4)</summary>

1. **Competition data is globally shared** — **confirmed:** every competition uses the same groups, awards, totals, and reward policy, while list counts remain stale after group CRUD. Scope these records by competition and derive or atomically update list metadata.
2. **Reward governance is bypassed** — **confirmed:** award submission ignores configured minimum/maximum values and posts immediately even when approval is required. Enforce limits and introduce a pending state that does not affect posted totals until approval.
3. **Competition detail is not keyboard accessible** — **confirmed:** neither list mode offers a focusable, keyboard-activated detail action, and opened dialogs do not contain or restore focus. Add semantic open controls plus modal focus trapping and restoration.
4. **Group deletion orphans award history** — **confirmed:** deleting a group leaves its awards in counts and logs but removes the group from filter choices and detail access. Block/archive groups with history or cascade and reconcile all dependent records explicitly.

</details>

<details><summary>Details</summary>

## Competition boundaries are absent from group and award state

`groups`, `awards`, and `rewards` are single state values at `CompetitionManagementPage.tsx:165-183`, and none of their records or selectors is keyed by the selected competition. Selection therefore changes the heading but not the managed dataset. This is directly visible for create: the new competition is stored with `groupCount: 0` and `participantCount: 0` at line 220, but opening it renders `groups.length`, member totals, point totals, and `awards.length` from the shared stores.

This also makes list metadata diverge after any group operation. Creating or deleting a group updates `groups` at lines 296 and 304 but never updates the selected competition’s `groupCount` or `participantCount`; returning to the list displays the original values. Scope groups, awards, logs, and reward configuration by competition and derive list counts from that scoped data (or update them atomically).

## Reward governance does not govern award submission

The Rewards tab exposes minimum and maximum awards, negative-point permission, and approval at `CompetitionManagementPage.tsx:403-404`. `handleAward` at lines 261-276 validates only `points > 0`, then immediately inserts an award and changes the group total. An administrator can configure a 5–500 range and still post 1 or 50,000 points (**confirmed**). With “Require approval” enabled, the same handler still posts the entry and updates totals immediately rather than holding it for review (**confirmed**).

The submission path needs to reject values outside the current range and model pending approval without changing posted totals or logs until approval. Otherwise the policy summary is misleading and cannot protect competition scores.

## Competition detail has no keyboard entry path

The default card view opens detail from an `onClick` on a non-interactive `<article>` at `CompetitionManagementPage.tsx:345`; compact mode repeats the pattern on a `<tr>` at line 357. Neither receives focus, handles Enter/Space, nor contains a dedicated “View” link/button. Edit and delete are keyboard reachable, but the detail containing group CRUD, award actions, logs, and reward configuration is not (**confirmed**).

Use a semantic link/button for the primary open action in each representation, preserving the nested edit/delete controls. The dialogs also identify themselves as modal at lines 130-139 but do not trap focus or restore it to the trigger; that should be corrected in the same accessibility pass (**confirmed**).

## Group deletion strands award history

`deleteGroup` at lines 303-306 filters only `groups`; `awards` remains unchanged. The award-log drawer renders those entries from the global award list, but its group selector is built from the remaining groups, so an orphaned group’s records remain in totals and search results without a selectable group filter (**confirmed**). The group-specific award dialog also becomes unreachable because its trigger existed only on the deleted group row.

Preserve auditability explicitly by blocking deletion for groups with history or archiving the group while retaining its filter identity. If deletion is intended to cascade, remove/reconcile its awards and totals in one operation and communicate the destructive scope in confirmation.
