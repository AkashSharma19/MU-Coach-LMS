# Channel-first announcement composer with targeted delivery

The implementation covers every requested channel combination, conditional content and previews, hierarchical audience selection, Mobile Push targeting, deterministic estimates, scheduling, and Review & send. It has no campaign name or campaign description fields. Push reach and recipient eligibility can contradict the selected targeting.

Watch for: **confirmed** Push estimates are produced for incompatible user/app combinations; **confirmed** recipient eligibility ignores cohort, term, section, and club; **confirmed** specific-recipient counts contradict the eligibility check and do not de-duplicate against the scoped audience.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

Channel-first selection, per-channel content, Push image/deep-link inputs, previews, scheduling, and final review match the requested flow.

Audience selection narrows through program, cohort, term, and section, with club/group as an additional filter. Mobile Push adds the requested role, app, and recipient controls, but its eligibility and reach calculations apply those dimensions inconsistently.

## Push estimates ignore user-to-app compatibility

**Confirmed:** `estimateAudience` multiplies an aggregate role factor by an aggregate device factor without preserving valid role/device relationships (`src/AnnouncementsPage.tsx:70-77`). Students + Admin App therefore reports eligible Push devices even though the modeled students have only Student App or no device; Staff + Student App has the inverse error. Derive aggregate reach from the same role/device associations used by the individual eligibility check, or constrain app choices by role.

## Recipient eligibility stops at program

**Confirmed:** the eligibility check verifies email existence, program, user type, and device type, while the selected audience can also narrow by cohort, term, section, and club (`src/AnnouncementsPage.tsx:406`). Recipient records do not contain those attributes, so the check cannot establish eligibility for a narrowed audience. Add the missing recipient attributes and evaluate every active hierarchy filter.

## Specific recipients use conflicting counting rules

**Confirmed:** Push reach adds selected recipients based on role and device without checking program, while the individual check rejects recipients outside the selected program (`src/AnnouncementsPage.tsx:73-80`, `src/AnnouncementsPage.tsx:406`). The saved `audienceCount` also adds every unique selected email to the aggregate audience without de-duplicating recipients already represented by that audience (`src/AnnouncementsPage.tsx:337`). Use one resolved eligible-recipient set for the check, estimated reach, review, and saved count.

</details>
